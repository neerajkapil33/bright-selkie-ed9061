Career advice is full of noise. We built W3M to cut straight to what's actually true about your situation.

Why "What's Wrong With Me?"

It's the question everyone quietly asks after another rejection, another ghosted application, another interview that didn't go anywhere. Usually the answer isn't "nothing" or "everything" — it's one or two specific, fixable things.

W3M exists to find those things quickly, say them plainly, and point you toward what to do next — without the vague platitudes most career advice defaults to.

3

Most people only need to fix around three things at a time. We focus the analysis there instead of overwhelming you with everything at once.

How it works

Three steps, start to finish.

1

Share your story

2

3

Fix what matters

A short, prioritized list of what to change first, so effort goes where it actually helps.
